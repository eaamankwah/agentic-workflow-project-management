## PRODUCT FEATURES

**Feature Name:** Message Classification Module  
**Description:** This feature allows the system to analyze email content using LLM-based classifiers to determine the intent and category of incoming messages.  
**Key Functionality:**  
1. Utilize LLM-based classifiers to analyze email content and identify the intent and category of each message.  
2. Assign confidence scores to determine whether automated responses or manual handling is required based on the analysis.  
**User Benefit:**  
- Users benefit from accurate categorization of incoming emails, ensuring that messages are routed to the appropriate handling process efficiently.  
- By assigning confidence scores, users can prioritize responses based on the level of automation or manual intervention required, optimizing workflow efficiency.

---

**Feature Name:** Response Generation Engine  
**Description:** This feature deploys a RAG system to generate contextually accurate and human-like responses. It includes an approval workflow for reviewing and editing automated responses before dispatch.  
**Key Functionality:**  
1. Utilizes a RAG system to generate contextually accurate responses based on email content.  
2. Provides an approval workflow for human review and editing of automated responses before sending.  
**User Benefit:**  
- Ensures that responses to incoming emails are relevant, accurate, and personalized, enhancing customer satisfaction.  
- Allows for human oversight to maintain consistency and quality in responses generated by the system.

---

**Feature Name:** Automated Email Classification  
**Description:** This feature involves the utilization of LLM-based classifiers to analyze email content and determine intent and category for efficient routing.  
**Key Functionality:**  
1. Implementation of LLM-based classifiers to analyze email content and determine intent and category.  
2. Assignment of confidence scores to decide between automated responses and manual handling.  
**User Benefit:**  
- Emails are categorized accurately and routed to the appropriate recipients swiftly, improving response times and efficiency.

---

**Feature Name:** Knowledge Base Integration

**Description:** This feature involves implementing a vector database within the Email Router system to efficiently store and retrieve organizational knowledge.

**Key Functionality:**
1. Store Organizational Knowledge: The system will store a wide range of information, including responses to common inquiries, best practices, and relevant documentation.
2. Retrieve Information: Users can easily search and retrieve relevant knowledge from the database to assist in responding to emails.
3. Continuous Learning: The system will continuously update the knowledge base with new information gathered from resolved inquiries, ensuring that it remains up-to-date and relevant.

**User Benefit:**
- Improved Efficiency: Users can quickly access and leverage existing knowledge to respond to inquiries, reducing response times and ensuring consistent messaging.
- Enhanced Accuracy: Access to a centralized knowledge base helps in providing accurate and reliable information to customers and partners.
- Time Savings: By having a repository of organizational knowledge readily available, users can focus on higher-value tasks rather than spending time searching for information.

---

Feature Name: Continuous Learning Mechanism
Description: This feature allows the system to update the knowledge base with new information derived from resolved inquiries.
Key Functionality: The system will analyze resolved inquiries to extract pertinent information and insights. It will then incorporate this new data into the knowledge base to enhance its accuracy and effectiveness over time.
User Benefit: Users will have access to an updated knowledge base that continuously enhances based on real-world interactions. This leads to more precise responses and improved decision-making capabilities.

---

**Feature Name:** Response Approval Workflow  
**Description:** This feature allows for the review and editing of automated responses before they are sent out to ensure accuracy and alignment with organizational standards.  
**Key Functionality:** Users can access a workflow where they can review the generated responses, make edits if necessary, and approve them for dispatch.  
**User Benefit:** By incorporating an approval workflow, users can ensure that the responses are contextually accurate, consistent with messaging standards, and meet the organization's requirements before being sent out, enhancing communication quality and customer satisfaction.

---

Feature Name: Automated Response Generation
Description: This feature allows the Email Router system to automatically generate responses for routine inquiries based on approved organizational knowledge, reducing the need for manual drafting.
Key Functionality:
- Utilizing a Response Generation Engine (RAG system) to create contextually accurate and human-like responses.
- Implementing an approval workflow for reviewing and editing automated responses before sending them out.
- Integrating with the knowledge base to access relevant information for crafting responses.
User Benefit:
- Saves time for staff members by automating responses to common inquiries, allowing them to focus on more complex tasks.
- Ensures consistent messaging and information delivery by standardizing responses to routine questions.
- Improves response times by providing quick and accurate replies to frequently asked questions.

---

**Feature Name:** User Interface  
**Description:** The User Interface of the Email Router system provides a dashboard for monitoring system performance and a configuration panel for managing the knowledge base, routing rules, and system settings.  
**Key Functionality:**  
1. Display performance metrics such as response times and accuracy on the dashboard for easy monitoring.  
2. Offer configuration options within the configuration panel to manage the knowledge base, routing rules, and system settings efficiently.  
3. Allow manual intervention to override automated processes when necessary, providing users with control over email handling.  
**User Benefit:**  
- Users can track system performance effectively through the dashboard, enabling timely adjustments and improvements.  
- The configuration panel facilitates easy management of key system components, enhancing operational efficiency.  
- Manual override capability ensures accurate handling of emails in situations where human intervention is required, improving overall email management quality.
